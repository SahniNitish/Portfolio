# LunchBox Express: Facebook Growth Strategy (one page)

**Goal:** more followers and engagement. Start with a small ad push, then **lower ad spend every month** until organic reach does the work.
**Budget cap:** **$150 CAD a month maximum, GST included.**
**Currency:** ₹ INR (Indian ad account), with CAD shown at **₹68 = 1 CAD**. Check a live rate first.
**Tax:** Meta adds **18% GST** on ad spend for Indian accounts without a GSTIN. Every budget below is *before* GST.

---

## 1. Ad budget: max $150 CAD a month (GST included), then taper

| Month | Daily (set in Ads Manager) | Monthly spend | + 18% GST | **Total paid (₹)** | **Total paid (CAD)** | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Launch** | ₹280 | ₹8,400 | ₹1,512 | **₹9,912** | **≈ $146** | test which Reels people like, build the first followers |
| **2 · Grow** | ₹200 | ₹6,000 | ₹1,080 | **₹7,080** | **≈ $104** | boost only proven winners |
| **3 · Taper** | ₹130 | ₹3,900 | ₹702 | **₹4,602** | **≈ $68** | best Reel of each week only |
| **4 · Maintain** | ₹70 | ₹2,100 | ₹378 | **₹2,478** | **≈ $36** | the new episode's trailer only |
| **5+ · Organic** | ₹0–70 | ₹0–2,100 | | ₹0–2,478 | $0–36 | organic reach carries the Page |
| **Total (4 months)** | | ₹20,400 | ₹3,672 | **₹24,072** | **≈ $354** | |

**Hard cap:** in Ads Manager, set an **account spending limit of ₹10,000** (Billing → Payment settings). Then you can never go over $150 CAD in a month, even by mistake.

**How each month's money is split:**
- **60%** boosts the best-performing Reel (Engagement objective). Aim for **under ₹1 per engagement** (about $0.015).
- **30%** goes to a **Page follow** campaign. Aim for **under ₹8 per follower** (about $0.12).
- **10%** tests new hooks and thumbnails.

**Rough results** (estimates only; real costs vary a lot by audience and creative): month 1 (₹8,400) could bring **about 800–2,500 new followers** plus thousands of engagements if most of the audience is in India. Targeting the US, Canada or UK brings fewer followers, at a higher cost each.

---

## 2. Who to target

- **Main audience:** India, ages 18–34, English. Interests: animation, short stories, mystery, sci-fi, plot twist, storytelling. Or let **Advantage+ audience** decide once you have about 1,000 followers.
- **Small test (10% of budget):** US, Canada and UK. Followers there cost more, but their views pay more if you join Facebook Content Monetization later.
- **Always exclude people who already follow the Page** from follow campaigns.

---

## 3. Ad rules (stops wasted money)

1. **Only boost posts that already work.** After 24–48 hours, boost the Reel with the best watch time and comments. Never boost a post nobody liked organically.
2. **Give each ad 3 days.** If its cost per result is more than 2× the target, stop it and try a new hook.
3. **First 2 seconds = the hook.** Open on the most shocking moment, with big captions. Most people watch on mute.
4. **Never buy followers or use "like for like" groups.** Fake followers kill your reach.
5. **No engagement bait** ("Like if…", "Share if…"). Facebook shows those posts to fewer people. Ask real questions instead.

---

## 4. Weekly posting plan (free reach)

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Text or image question ("Your phone shows tomorrow's news. What's the first thing you check?") |
| Tue | **Reel**: a cliffhanger clip from the current episode |
| Wed | Behind the scenes: a character sketch, "which design do you like?" (great for comments) |
| Thu | **Reel**: the best twist moment |
| Fri | **Full episode** (16:9 video), pinned to the top of the Page |
| Sat | **Reel**: a funny or emotional moment, or a "Part 2 is out" teaser |
| Sun | Poll or "Guess the ending" post |

- **When to post:** **7–10 PM IST**, at the same time every day.
- **First hour after posting:** reply to **every** comment, and pin the best comment.
- **Cross-promote:** share each Page post to your own profile once. Put the Facebook link in YouTube descriptions, and the YouTube link in Facebook comments, never in the post itself.

---

## 5. What to measure every Sunday

| Number | Target |
|---|---|
| New followers this week | rising each week |
| Engagement rate (reactions + comments + shares ÷ reach) | **3%+** |
| Reels: average watch time | **> 50%** of the clip length |
| Cost per follower (ads) | **< ₹8** (about $0.12) |
| Cost per engagement (ads) | **< ₹1** (about $0.015) |
| Share of reach from organic | rising each month, so you can cut ads |

**When to cut ads early:** once **organic reach is more than 70% of total reach** for 2 weeks in a row, jump straight to the next lower budget row.

---

## 6. Monetization checkpoints

- **Facebook Content Monetization** (Professional Dashboard → Monetization): follow the eligibility steps shown there. They need consistent original posting plus enough followers and engagement.
- **Stars and subscriptions** unlock as the audience grows. Turn them on as soon as the dashboard offers them.
- **Keep everything original:** your own stories and art. Reposted or copied content blocks monetization.
