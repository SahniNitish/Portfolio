# EP02: What If You Woke Up 1,000 Years in the Future?

**Logline:** You sign up for a one-year sleep experiment in 2026. You wake up in 3026, the only person alive from "the Before."
**Runtime:** about 10 min · **HUD:** `YEAR` 3026 (left) / `PEOPLE WHO UNDERSTAND YOU` (right, starts at 0)
**Emotional arc:** confusion → wonder → celebrity → being a museum piece → mystery
**Series hook:** ends on a cliffhanger that sets up EP02 Part 2.

## Facts to check
- English around the year 1026 was Old English (before the Norman Conquest of 1066). Lord's Prayer line, standard Old English text.
- 1,000 years ≈ 40 generations at about 25 years per generation.
- Everything about 3026 is fiction. Say it as story, never as prediction.
