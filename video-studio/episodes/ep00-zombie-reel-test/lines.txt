Day one. The radio said stay indoors. I was buying snacks at a gas station when the power cut out.
Then I saw them. Dozens of people, shuffling down the highway. Grey skin. Empty eyes.
I locked the glass doors and dragged a shelf in front of them. The clerk had already run out the back.
I counted my supplies. Forty bags of chips, twelve energy drinks, and one flashlight.
Day three. They know I'm in here. They press their faces against the glass all night.
Day five. My flashlight landed on a trapdoor in the back room. It was locked... from the inside.
