# EP01: What If You Could Never Sleep Again?

**Logline:** One night your body forgets how to sleep. You get a third of your life back for free, and slowly find out what sleep was actually for.
**Runtime:** about 10 min (about 1,700 words) · **HUD:** `HOURS AWAKE` (left) / `ENERGY` (right, always 100%, which becomes the joke)
**Emotional arc:** wonder → advantage → isolation → acceptance → one last gift

> Fix from the pilot: the cold-open close-up shows a wrecked face, but rule 1 says you never get tired. The narration now covers that: *"Your body isn't tired. You are."*

## Facts to check before recording
- About a third of life spent asleep (general sleep-science figure: about 8 of every 24 hours).
- Randy Gardner, about 264 hours awake, Dec 1963 – Jan 1964 (Guinness later stopped tracking the category).
- Glymphatic clearance during sleep: Xie L. et al., *Science* 2013 (shown in mice).
