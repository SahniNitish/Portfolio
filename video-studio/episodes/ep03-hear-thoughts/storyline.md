# EP03: What If You Could Hear Everyone's Thoughts?

**Logline:** You wake up hearing your mum's thoughts. Every day the range grows ten times, until you can hear every mind on Earth.
**Runtime:** about 10 min · **HUD:** `PEOPLE IN YOUR HEAD` (left, ×10 each step) / `SECRETS KNOWN` (right)
**Emotional arc:** fun → power → overload → hiding → understanding → dread
**Why it works:** the ladder is a perfect ×10: 1 → 10 → 100 → … → 8 billion, which fits the 12-step bar exactly.

## Facts to check
- Mind-wandering about 46.9% of waking time: Killingsworth & Gilbert, *Science* 2010.
- About 6,200 "thought transitions" a day: Tseng & Poppenk, *Nature Communications* 2020 (present it as "around 6,000 by one estimate").
- World population about 8.2 billion in 2026; round to "eight billion" in narration.
