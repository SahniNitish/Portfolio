# Phase A: director's proposal, 20-second sample (Style 4 Cozy Storytelling, 16:9, 2 clips x 10 s)

Title: "Why School Feels Pointless" (topic: Prof. Jiang Xueqin's Predictive History; script original)
Core message: school pays for grades, not learning. Change the reward and the school changes.
Voice (frozen for both clips): adult female, General American, clear warm mid-high voice, gentle forward resonance, no rasp or breathiness, lively conversational delivery with controlled pitch variation, steady pace, close dry studio recording. About 41 English words, about 17 s of speech.
Palette: dull grey-green (the pointless school) turning warm gold and sky blue (the changed school). Accents: red = grades, green = learning, gold = reward.
Music: curious light pizzicato and soft marimba, same motif in both clips, brightening in clip 2.

Cast: ONE protagonist, a student. White filled round head, two small dark oval eyes, eyebrow strokes, short curved mouth; slim white torso in a dark outline; single-line dark arms and legs; no hair, no accessories. (Backpack allowed as a prop, not part of the body.)
Props: a gold-star sticker ribbon (stands for grades, no text), an open book.

CLIP 1 (0-10 s), SETTING: a dull grey-green classroom, then a school hallway.
 [0-3s] Student slumped at a desk, three question-mark shapes float up. VO: "Why does school feel pointless?"
 [3-7s] A gold-star ribbon drops onto the desk, coins rain on it; the open book beside it gathers dust. VO: "Because it pays for grades, not learning."
 [7-10s] Cut to the hallway: the student runs after a floating gold star, leaving the book on the floor. VO: "So students chase the A, and skip the answer."
CLIP 2 (10-20 s), SETTING: a bright sunny school courtyard.
 [10-13s] Student stops, picks the book up, looks at the star drifting away. VO: "Change the reward..."
 [13-17s] A big lever by the school gate flips from a star icon to an open-book icon; the grey school building floods with warm colour. VO: "...and the whole school changes."
 [17-20s] Student and classmates (the only extra cast: two simple matching students) cheer in the courtyard, camera pulls back. VO: "School isn't broken. It's a game."

Cut/time notes: classroom to hallway = same student, same screen direction (running right); clip 1 to clip 2 = student skids to a stop, then a new location (named location cut).
Post-production: captions word by word, sound effects, joining the clips.
