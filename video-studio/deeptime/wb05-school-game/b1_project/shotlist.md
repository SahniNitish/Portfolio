## Shot 40: lines 46-48
Idea: lines 46-48: reformer pushed offscreen
Narration: So why did the Shenzhen reformer fail? Because he tried to impose a better game on players who hadn't agreed to play it. Strict fairness threatened hidden privileges. Harder learning created more work. New responsibilities disrupted established hierarchies. And record admissions couldn't save him from the stakeholders whose support he needed.

## Shot 41: lines 49-52
Idea: lines 49-52: sustainable reform = overlap of interests, small steps
Narration: Sustainable reform doesn't begin with a heroic outsider rewriting every rule. It begins inside the overlap between existing interests. Give parents visible evidence of real growth. Give teachers reforms they can actually sustain. Give administrators wins that don't threaten their survival. Give students meaningful challenges, and reasons to value them beyond a grade. Then move the equilibrium, one step at a time.

## Shot 42: lines 53-54
Idea: lines 53-54: ask a better question
Narration: So the next time you ask, why doesn't school create curious, independent thinkers? Ask a better question. Who gets rewarded when students truly learn? Who pays the price? And who has the power to stop it?
