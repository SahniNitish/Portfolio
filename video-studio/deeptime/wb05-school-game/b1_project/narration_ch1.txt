School has three simple jobs. Teach us to read. Teach us to think. And teach us to keep learning for the rest of our lives. So why do schools often produce memorization instead of thinking... competition instead of collaboration... And students who celebrate the end of exams by never wanting to open a book again? What if school isn't broken? What if it's working exactly as the system rewards it to work? To understand that, let's look at the man who built the school everyone claims to want... Got students into Yale, Wharton, and Cornell... And then got fired.
