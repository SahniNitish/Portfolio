# Why is school this way? 30-second prompt pack for Google Flow (Gemini Omni Flash)

Style 4 Cozy Storytelling, 16:9, 3 clips x 10 s, one protagonist, one female narrator. Paste each prompt on its own into Flow; each is complete by itself.

## Global continuity block
- Ratio/length: 16:9, 30 s (3 clips).
- Story: school looks like a factory because it was built that way, runs on rewards today, and can be changed by changing the rewards.
- Character: the one protagonist anchor below, repeated word for word in every clip.
- New setting each clip: old factory hall, then exam hall with the running wheel, then sunny courtyard. Cuts connect through the protagonist, the gold star, and the movement direction (left to right).
- Narrator: one female voice block, repeated word for word. Score: pizzicato + marimba theme from clip 1.
- Total narration: about 66 English words.

## CLIP 1 PROMPT

Output: one standalone clip of approximately ten seconds, 16:9 widescreen, 720p, 24 FPS, synchronized audio.

Setting: a huge old brick factory hall with a high iron roof, long belts and rows of identical wooden desks bolted to a moving platform, dusty window light. Fixed furniture: the desks and a tall factory bell hanging over the hall.

Character lock: One minimalist 2D cartoon protagonist with a white filled circular head, two small dark oval eyes, simple eyebrow strokes and a short curved mouth. The torso is a slim closed tapered shape with solid white fill inside its dark outline. Both arms and both legs are dark single-line strokes with constant medium weight; hands are small dark rounded shapes and feet are short dark rounded strokes. No hair or clothing accessories. Keep the same head-to-body proportions, white head and torso fill, and outline weight in every shot. Exactly two arms and two legs exist; a moving limb replaces its previous pose rather than leaving another limb behind. Expressions change only the simple facial marks, never the head shape or character design.

Palette: dull grey-green and muted brown for the pointless school, warm gold and sky blue only where the story brightens; vivid red marks grades, soft green marks learning, warm gold marks reward.

Composition: stage the action across the left, centre and right of the wide frame, with gentle lateral camera moves and clear negative space; vary wide, medium and close shots about every two to three seconds.

First frame: the protagonist sits in the middle desk of the front row, arms resting on the desk, a small question-mark shape floating above its head.

[0-3s] Wide shot: the factory bell swings and rings; the platform with all desks slides forward in a perfectly straight row while the protagonist tilts its head, puzzled, and the question-mark shape pops above it.

[3-7s] Medium tracking shot: a conveyor belt of identical grey blank pages and gold-star stickers rolls past; the protagonist lifts one arm, a stamp drops onto its desk and a gold star appears, then the next desk gets an identical stamp.

[7-10s] Close-up then pull-back: the protagonist grabs the edge of the desk as the platform lurches, the bell rings again, the whole row of desks rolls through a large doorway toward bright light.

Audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes. The narrator speaks this exact line once, with no words added, omitted, paraphrased, repeated or reordered, and nothing captioned or shown as text: "Why is school built this way? Bells, rows, and grades. Many trace it to the 1800s, when schools were designed to train obedient workers."

Voice lock: One off-screen adult female narrator throughout. General American accent, clear warm mid-high voice, gentle forward resonance, no raspiness or breathiness. Lively conversational delivery with controlled pitch variation, steady speaking pace, and close, dry studio sound. Keep the same vocal timbre, register, accent, pace, and recording quality throughout. Emotional progression belongs to the visuals and music; do not change the narrator's voice or switch to character dialogue.

Music and SFX: a curious light pizzicato and soft marimba theme establishes the score in this clip, mixed under the narration (voice first), with crisp synchronized sound effects on every physical action.

Final frame: the protagonist rides the sliding desk through the doorway, looking forward with wide eyes, the gold-star sticker on the desk edge.

Negative: no visible words, letters, numbers, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks anywhere (icons and shapes only); no speech bubbles or dialogue boxes; no extra characters beyond those described; no extra or duplicated limbs; no hair or accessories on the protagonist; no change to the head shape or character design; no liquid or melting shape morphing; no photorealistic or 3D humans; no sketchbook borders or paper overlays.

## Stitching guide
- Join clip 1 to 2 at the moment the desk passes through the doorway and the frame whites out slightly; join 2 to 3 where the wheel slows and the protagonist steps off. Trim 0.2 to 0.5 s from the start of clips 2 and 3 if a frame repeats.
- Generate each clip 2 to 3 times and keep the one where the protagonist looks identical (white head, white torso, dark stick limbs, no hair).
- Add captions in an editor, not in the prompt. Overlay ideas (2 to 5 words): "BELLS. ROWS. GRADES.", "CHASE THE A", "CHANGE THE REWARD".

## Voice and music note
- The female voice block and the music wording are identical in all three prompts. If clips sound like different people, compare the voice blocks for exact equality first, then retry only the weakest clip.
- Facts to check before posting: modern mass schooling is often traced to 19th-century Prussia; historians debate how much of it was meant to train workers, so the line says "many trace it".
